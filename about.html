About Hotel Paradise

A simple academic frontend for hotel and resort management.

Our Project

Hotel Paradise is a fictional hotel used for the Hotel and Resort Management System academic project. The interface focuses on organizing rooms, guests, reservations, check-in and check-out information and billing.

Customer Side

Customers can view rooms, room details and submit booking information.

Staff Side

Authorized staff pages show reservations, guests, room status and stay operations.

Admin Side

Administrative pages organize guest information, billing and basic reports.
